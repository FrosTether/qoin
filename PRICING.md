# Qoin Maker pricing

Source-available core. Paid Production Use. Designed so licensing is the
business model, not ads or hidden token taxes.

## List prices (2026)

| Tier | Price | Best for | Production cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community | $0 | Learning, audits of the generator, tiny experiments | Trailing revenue from generated tokens under $10k / year |
| Starter | **$149** once | One serious token launch | 1 named project, 1 EVM chain |
| Studio | **$499 / year** | Founders shipping several tokens | Unlimited own-brand projects, 5 chains, 3 seats |
| Agency | **$2,490 / year** | Studios launching tokens for clients | Unlimited client projects, white-label, 15 seats |
| Enterprise | **from $8,000 / year** | Exchanges, fintech, governments | Custom paper, SSO, indemnification negotiation, audit pack |

## What makes this profitable (and fair)

1. **The generator is visible.** Buyers can inspect templates before they pay.
   That increases trust and conversion versus a closed binary.
2. **The restriction is on Production Use**, not on reading code. Researchers
   and hobbyists are not the customer. Agencies and launchpads are.
3. **Change Date (2030-09-26)** converts each published version to Apache-2.0.
   That is a credible promise and still leaves years of paid runway.
4. **Starter is cheap on purpose.** One paid launch covers many hours of
   support and funds the next template (Permit2, Base-specific gas notes,
   vesting wallets).
5. **Agency is the margin tier.** White-label + client work is where token
   factories actually make money. Price it like a plugin license, not like
   an audit.

## Revenue hygiene

- Never take a cut of a customer's token supply as "license payment".
- Never ship tax/honeypot templates under this brand.
- Keep invoices and license grants in writing.
- If you fork this repo to sell licenses yourself, you need an Agency or
  Enterprise grant from the original Licensor — that is the white-label path.

## Discount policy

- Education / registered non-profit: 100% off Community (already free) or
  50% off Starter with proof.
- No lifetime-unlimited public coupons. They destroy Agency conversions.
- Enterprise pricing may include a year-one pilot at Studio + success fee.

Update the contact path in `COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md` before advertising these
numbers publicly.
