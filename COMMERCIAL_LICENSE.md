# Qoin Maker Commercial License

This document is the **offer and term sheet** for paid Production Use of Qoin Maker.
A binding license is formed when Licensor accepts payment (or issues a signed order)
and delivers a license key / confirmation email.

Not affiliated with Qoin (qoin.com), the former Qoin Foundation, or Post-Quantum Qoin.

## Why this exists

The community grant in `LICENSE` covers evaluation, education, and very small
experiments. The moment Qoin Maker is used to launch client tokens, run an
agency, white-label a factory, or operate a commercial mint, that work should
pay for continued development, audits, and support.

This is how the project stays profitable without locking the source away.

## How to buy

   or open a GitHub issue with the subject `Commercial license request`.
2. Include: legal name, country, intended use, tier, number of seats.
3. Licensor issues an invoice (Stripe, Wise, or crypto on a major L2).
4. After payment, you receive:
   - license confirmation PDF
   - license key (optional enforcement in future CLI builds)
   - support mailbox

Until a real payments mailbox is wired, treat GitHub Issues on this repo
as the official intake.

## Restrictions that survive a paid license

A Commercial License does **not** allow you to:

- Relicense Qoin Maker itself under an OSI license
- Publish the generator as a competing open factory without a white-label add-on
- Claim audits, legal opinions, or listings that Licensor did not provide
- Generate hidden-fee, honeypot, or blacklist-drain contracts using this brand
  (those templates are intentionally not shipped)
